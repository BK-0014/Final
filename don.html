THE STORY

Don Toliver, whose real name is Caleb Zackery Toliver, is an American rapper, singer, and songwriter born on June 12, 1994, in Houston, Texas. He grew up in the Alief neighborhood, where music played a major role in his life. His father was also a musician, which helped inspire his passion for music at an early age. Don began recording songs as a teenager and worked on building his career through independent music releases.

His big breakthrough came in 2018 when he was featured on Travis Scott’s album ASTROWORLD on the song “CAN’T SAY.” He later signed with Travis Scott’s Cactus Jack Records and gained worldwide recognition with hit songs like “No Idea,” “After Party,” and “Lemonade.” Over the years, he has developed a unique sound that blends hip-hop, trap, and R&B, becoming known for his melodic voice and creative style.

Don Toliver is continuing to build his career as one of hip-hop’s biggest rising stars. His fifth studio album, OCTANE, released in January 2026, became his first No. 1 album, helping him reach an even larger audience. He has also received five Grammy nominations this year, including Best New Artist.

Currently, he is preparing for the release of his next album, NITROUS, scheduled for December 4, 2026. He recently released a new single called “Party With the Boys,” which will appear on the upcoming album. Don is also expanding his work as a producer and songwriter, showing that he wants to grow beyond being just a rapper and become an even bigger force in the music industry.
